01What exactly does Fleapo build?

We build AI-powered products end-to-end — from strategy and architecture to live deployment. This includes AI-native applications, agentic backends that automate operations, consumer platforms, and legacy system upgrades. If it involves AI and production-grade code, we build it.

02How long does a typical project take?

Most builds go from kickoff to production in 4–8 weeks. We start with LeapMap — a 1–2 week audit that identifies your highest-ROI AI opportunities — then move directly into LeapBuild with weekly shippable increments. You see working product, not just slide decks.

03Do I need a technical team to work with Fleapo?

No. We function as your entire AI engineering team — strategy, architecture, design, build, testing, and deployment. We embed directly into your operations and work as an extension of your leadership team, not a vendor you chase for updates.

04What stage of company is Fleapo best suited for?

We work with seed-stage startups through to Series B companies, as well as enterprises looking to deploy AI at scale. We have specific engagement models for each stage — from quick agentic prototypes for early-stage founders to full AI transformation builds for growth-stage companies.

05What makes Fleapo different from a regular software agency or AI consultancy?

Most agencies build what you ask for. Most consultancies tell you what to build. We do both — and we ship it. We're not a feature vendor bolting AI onto the side of your product. We build AI into the core of it, from the first sprint. No 12-month retainers. If we don't deliver, you don't pay.

06How do you charge?

We start with a fixed-fee AI Opportunity Sprint — 2 weeks, 5 prioritised use cases, a roadmap sized by revenue impact. From there, engagements are scoped per project. No lock-in contracts. No bloated retainers. We earn the relationship every sprint.
