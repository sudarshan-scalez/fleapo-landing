01What is the AI Readiness Index?

It's a structured assessment that benchmarks how AI is actually used across your leadership and teams. It produces a readiness score, function-level benchmarks, and a prioritised roadmap showing where AI will create the most value first.

02Do we have to start with the AI Readiness Index?

No — but starting with measurement gives leadership a clear, evidence-based roadmap before any enablement or build work begins. Most organisations find it removes the guesswork and the internal debate.

03Is this an AI training course?

No. We don't run generic, pre-recorded courses. Our enablement happens inside your real workflows, with your real teams, and is followed by building the actual systems that make the change stick.

04What kinds of AI systems do you build?

Internal tools, AI agents, workflow automation, knowledge systems, and operational pipelines — embedded into how your organisation already works. It's the same production engineering behind the 150+ products we've shipped.

05How long does an AI transformation take?

It depends on scope, but engagements move in clear stages — diagnosis, then team enablement, then building infrastructure — across defined milestones rather than one open-ended program. You see value at each stage, not only at the end.

06How are you different from a consulting firm?

Consultants hand you a deck and leave. We measure, enable your teams, and then build and deploy the AI systems ourselves — and we've shipped real products at scale. You get working infrastructure, not just recommendations.
